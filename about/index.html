Our story

From a small Abids workshop to Hyderabad's premium signage brand

Grafick Signs started in 2014 as a one-person workshop in Abids, Hyderabad. We fabricated our first neon sign for a local boutique, and word-of-mouth quickly spread.

Over 10+ years, we've grown into a full-service signage studio with in-house fabrication of 3D letters, neon signs, ACP cladding, channel letters, and more. We've delivered 500+ projects for cafes, restaurants, hotels, IT parks, retail brands, and corporate offices.

What hasn't changed: every project is still fabricated by hand in our workshop, and installed by our own team. No middlemen, no outsourcing, no surprises.

What we stand for

Our values

Why Hyderabad businesses choose Grafick — and keep coming back.

No middlemen

Design, fabrication, and installation — all done by our own team. The person who measures your site is the same person who fabricates your sign.

Premium materials

We use only branded materials: Osram LEDs, Alstrong ACP, SS 304/316, and UV-stable acrylic. No knockoffs, no shortcuts.

On-time delivery

We commit to a deadline and stick to it. Most projects are live within 5-10 days. Rush orders accommodated.

Real warranty

1-year warranty on all electrical work. 5-year LED warranty. 10-year anti-fade on ACP. We come back and fix issues — no questions asked.

Fixed-price quotes

The price we quote is the price you pay. No hidden costs, no surprise add-ons. Site visit, fabrication, install — all included.

Direct communication

Talk directly to our team on WhatsApp. No account managers, no ticket systems. Quick responses, real conversations.
